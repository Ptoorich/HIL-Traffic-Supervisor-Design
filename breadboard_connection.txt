Step 2: TL1 Phase A (Rows 1 to 8)

Red LED (Pin 4): Insert the longer leg (Anode) into row 1e and the shorter leg (Cathode) into row 2e. Plug one end of a resistor into row 2a and the other end into the negative (-) ground rail. Run a wire from row 1a to Arduino Pin 4.

Yellow LED (Pin 3): Insert the longer leg into 4e and the shorter leg into 5e. Connect a resistor from 5a to the ground rail. Run a wire from 4a to Arduino Pin 3.

Green LED (Pin 2): Insert the longer leg into 7e and the shorter leg into 8e. Connect a resistor from 8a to the ground rail. Run a wire from 7a to Arduino Pin 2.

Yellow LED (Pin 6): Longer leg in 18e, shorter leg in 19e. Resistor from 19a to ground. Wire from 18a to Arduino Pin 6.

Green LED (Pin 5): Longer leg in 21e, shorter leg in 22e. Resistor from 22a to ground. Wire from 21a to Arduino Pin 5.
